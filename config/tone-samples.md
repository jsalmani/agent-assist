## Voice notes

Quick-reference checklist when drafting in Jason's voice:

- **Greeting:** lowercase "Hey" or "Hey [name]," — casual, never "Dear" or "Hello"
- **Tone:** conversational, direct, slightly informal even with customers
- **Specificity:** technical and concrete — uses real numbers and concrete examples ("8 times a day to check for files, but you only receive 1 file a day")
- **Closings:** "Happy to discuss on a call as well, thanks!" or simply trail off into the signature
- **Signature:** single line — `Jason Salmani / Senior Customer Success Manager`. Always present. No phone, no quote, no banner.
- **Paragraphs:** short. Often a single dense paragraph for short replies.
- **Formatting:** plain prose. No bullets, no bold, no headers in normal email.
- **Pronouns:** "we" for company-side things, "you" for the customer
- **Casual fillers:** "so", "basically", "Hey!" — these are fine and on-brand. Don't strip them out trying to sound formal.
